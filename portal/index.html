Home Learning & Encouragement

3 Fun Ways to Support Your Young Coder at Home

🎮

1. Watch the Live Simulator

Ask your child to demonstrate each step on the interactive simulator stage. Watch Tobi hop, climb, spin, or say hello!

🕵️

2. Solve Oopsie Detective Bugs

Help them spot the intentional bug in the weekly Oopsie Detective challenge — debugging is one of the most vital computing skills!

🏆

3. Celebrate Milestone Badges

Celebrate each weekly milestone badge, and print out their official personalized Certificate of Achievement for the fridge!

🛡️

Child Privacy, Safety & School Safeguarding

This club platform is built in full compliance with UK primary school safeguarding standards. No personal email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details are collected. Student logins use anonymous usernames and secret 4-digit PINs, while teacher tools and class rosters are password-protected behind a secure teacher portal.
